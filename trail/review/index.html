Work puzzles (27)

One per WORK WEEK tap, Professor Layton style, Uniswap engineering. The ✓ marks the answer; players never see it. Solved clean: +$100 a day for the week; with the hint: +$25; wrong twice: -$75. Text lives in src/data/puzzles.json and the Obsidian copy note.

Bots and swaps type a number · pool ▶ play

5 trading bots can fill 5 swaps in 5 minutes. How many bots does it take to fill 100 swaps in 100 minutes?

Answer: 5

Hint: Work out how many swaps ONE bot fills in 5 minutes, then in 100 minutes.

Explain: 5. One bot fills one swap every 5 minutes, so 20 swaps in 100 minutes. Five bots fill 100. The big numbers were a costume.

Ten laptops type a number · code ▶ play

Ten laptops sit on the office table on battery, all running the test suite. Before leaving you close two of them. Later a colleague closes one more. The rest run all night. In the morning, how many laptops still have battery?

Answer: 3

Hint: What happens to a laptop that runs on battery all night?

Explain: 3. The seven that kept running drained flat. Only the three that were closed still have charge.

Splitting the port type a number · code ▶ play

Two engineers must port 10 modules to the new framework. They split the list in half and start at opposite ends. Ana ports 1 module an hour, Ben ports 2. When both are done, what percent of the modules did Ana port?

Answer: 50

Hint: Who decided how many pools each person does? Read the second sentence.

Explain: 50 percent. They split the list in half before starting. Speed never came into it; Ben just finished first and went for coffee.

The last letter pick one · whiteboard ▶ play

The first letter of Uniswap is U and the second is N. But the letter you should worry about is the last one. What is the last letter of the alphabet?

  1. Z
  2. T ✓
  3. U
  4. P

Hint: Read the question again, very literally.

Explain: T. The last letter of the word 'alphabet'. Not Z. Reading the whole ticket is most of the job.

The doubling log type a number · code ▶ play

A bug makes one log line copy itself every minute: 1 line, then 2, then 4, and so on. After 60 minutes the disk is full. After how many minutes was the disk half full?

Answer: 59

Hint: It doubles every minute. Go backwards from full.

Explain: 59. If it doubles each minute, half full is one minute before full. The disk looked fine for nearly an hour and then it did not.

The wallet and the case type a number · whiteboard ▶ play

A hardware wallet and its case cost $110 together. The wallet costs $100 more than the case. How much is the case?

Answer: 5

Hint: Not $10. Check: is the wallet exactly $100 more than the case?

Explain: $5. Case 5, wallet 105: together 110, and the difference is exactly 100. Almost everybody says 10 first.

Three wallets pick one · whiteboard ▶ play

Three traders leave identical hardware wallets on the desk and each grabs one without looking. What is the chance that exactly two of them pick up their own wallet?

  1. 1 in 6
  2. 0, it cannot happen ✓
  3. 1 in 2
  4. 1 in 3

Hint: If two have their own, whose wallet is left for the third?

Explain: Zero. If two people hold their own wallets, the only one left belongs to the third person. Exactly two is impossible.

Half of itself type a number · pool ▶ play

A trader asks how much ETH the pool holds. The answer: 'As much as your wallet, plus half of the pool itself.' Your wallet holds 22 ETH. How much ETH is in the pool?

Answer: 44

Hint: Your 22 ETH must be the other half of the pool.

Explain: 44 ETH. If the pool is your 22 plus half of itself, your 22 is the other half. So the pool is 44.

Breaking the bar type a number · whiteboard ▶ play

A chocolate bar from the corner shop is a sheet of 30 squares. Each break splits one piece in two along the lines. How many breaks to get 30 single squares?
