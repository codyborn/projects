# Mini-games

Self-contained Phaser scenes (Cooking, Workout, CarryOn console, Kite, Airport, Laundry, Drone, Work). Contract: start with `scene.start(KEY, launch)` where `launch: MinigameLaunch = { energy, difficulty, payload, onDone }`.
Each scene shows a 1s READY card, runs, shows a 1.5s PERFECT / NICE / FAILED card, calls `onDone({ score 0..100, perfect: score>=95, failed: score<50 || forced })` exactly once, then stops itself. Never starts another scene.

**No more command words (round 10, Cody: "remove CHAIN! etc."):** nothing displays `META[id].word` any more (it stays for the harness). Every micro has a short `META[id].hint` (≤ 32 chars) that the pre-game card shows under the instruction and that `frame.setHint()` keeps in the HUD strip's centre slot (8 px) for the whole game; games with phases (burpee, boulder beta) overwrite it with the phase cue; Stretch no longer resets when the finger lifts: the slider slips back 25%/s toward the pass start until you press again, and a jerk costs 30% of the pass instead of the whole slider. **Result cards (round 10):** `MinigameFrame.finish()` shows the result (PERFECT / NICE / FAILED, the score) and HOLDS it until the player taps CONTINUE (200×48 button, SPACE/ENTER too); `onDone` fires only then, once, and the scene stops. Nothing auto-advances after an activity. Cooking passes `{ y: 470, keep: [...] }` so the big dish reveal stays above the overlay; workout sessions show each game's result with CONTINUE (`frame.interlude()`, clock paused) before the next game's READY card. The harness presses CONTINUE for every scene every 200 ms unless `?holdResult=1`. **READY cards (round 9):** `MinigameFrame.intro()` shows an instruction card with a READY button and waits; the play clock and cap start on READY. Cooking's card shows the dish, ingredients, the finished art and the step words; Workout's first command card has a READY button (round cards stay tap-to-skip); Airport and Kite draw their own full cards and pass `{ auto: true }`; CarryOn calls `frame.beginPlay()` from its own START. `frame.ready()` presses READY for harnesses.

**Lives (round 3):** outdoor Workout variants (trailrun, hike, ferrata, boulder) start with 1 life + `launch.extraLives` (hiking boots give +1), drawn as pixel hearts under the HUD strip. trailrun: a rock costs a life instead of only score; hike: every third dizzy warning costs a life; boulder: empty grip costs a life and refills to 60%; ferrata: a fall below the last anchor. Losing the last life ends the game as FAILED with a partial score (≤ 45). bands and swim have no lives.

**Play cap (round 2):** `MinigameFrame.capSec` (default 36 s of play; the result card then holds until CONTINUE) auto-finishes any game with `frame.scoreNow()`, which every game sets to its live score. Coin Collector overrides the cap to 60 s. Games with their own timers are shorter: Laundry 20 s, Trail run 25 s, Hike 30 s, Ferrata 35 s, Kite 35 s, Airport ~29 s.

Energy < 50 makes every game harder through `MinigameFrame` (`_shared.ts`): tighter timing windows (`frame.window`), input lag up to 140 ms (`frame.lag`), a slow camera wobble, and per-game drains (grip, pace, wind). `difficulty` 0..1 speeds things up.

## Level design for Coin Collector (game id `carryon`)
`carryonLevel.ts` holds `DEFAULT_LEVEL`, the physics constants (`PHYS`: jump 310, gravity 900, run 115 px/s) and `validateLevel(level)`, a BFS reachability check using the real jump kinematics (max rise 3 rows; horizontal reach 3 tiles for a 3-row rise, 4 for lower). Levels are 23×20 tiles of 16 px. Keep every rise ≤ 3 rows and gaps ≤ 3 tiles; run `npm test` (vitest, `carryonLevel.test.ts`) to prove a level is completable. The scene also logs `console.warn` for a bad level at runtime.

## Dish steps
Every dish in `src/data/dishes.json` uses 4 to 5 steps that make culinary sense (ramen: knead, simmer, pour, plate; tacos al pastor: grill, dice, fold, season; sushi: shake, roll, dice, plate), and no two dishes in the same city share a step sequence. `dishArt.test.ts` enforces: a spec per dish, 4 to 5 known steps, per-city uniqueness, and that all 15 kinds are used somewhere. Round 9 split chop (tap, dicing/hacking: onion, herbs, cabbage, pastor meat) from slice (drag along a line: sashimi, ceviche fish, lox, tomato, bread, carving, cheese, soba sheet).

## Dev harness

Console extras: `?auto=1&fast=1&only=CarryOn&limit=3` filters the auto run; `?console=carryon|tetris&city=&cityName=&hazard=&seed=` boots one cartridge in real time for screenshots (the page lives under the `/trail/` base: `http://localhost:5174/trail/harness.html`). Kite: `?kite=rt` presses READY and lets the run finish on its own; `?kite=perfect&fast=1` scripts hold-in-glow → release on a crest → press mid-air only when `hint().dropLand` (the predicted swell phase at touchdown under a fast drop) is above 0.3, and expects clean landings with no wipeouts. Knife steps: `?chop=1(&n=6)` taps evenly and expects 100; `?slice=aligned|off` strokes on the line (high) or 26 px beside it (low); `?chopslice=1` plays the completo (chop + slice) and skips the other steps with `endStep(1)`, expecting onDone once; the driver flips the title to SHOT_CHOP / SHOT_SLICE mid-step for screenshots. Shake: `?shake=motion|swipe(&n=6)` presses READY, then either dispatches synthetic `DeviceMotionEvent`s (alternating ±16 m/s²) or none, and reports the modes the step went through and the detector's count. City Run: `?cityrun=safe|random|none(&fast=1&hitboxes=1)` scripts a Frogger crossing through `hint()/act()` (hops only into lanes clear for the hop), mashes random arrow keys through the Pad, or gives no input (the crossing clock takes the lives; reports when the first was lost).
`devHarness.ts` exports `MINIGAME_SCENES` and `launchHarness(game)` (adds a `MinigameHarness` picker with sample payloads and an energy toggle; integrator wires it under `?harness=1`). `harness-entry.ts` is a standalone entry used with a `harness.html` page: `?auto=1&fast=1` drives all six games with synthetic input under a virtual clock and writes results (incl. `vms` = virtual game ms per run) to `#results` (round 4: 35 runs incl. one per new cooking step kind and four full dishes, one `onDone` each, zero page errors; it also renders all 49 dish arts first and records the count in `window.__dishArtRendered`). `?sheet=1&ids=a,b,c` draws a contact sheet of finished dishes into `#sheet`.

## Known limitations
- Art is placeholder-procedural (rectangles, generated 16 px textures); the ART agent can swap textures by key (`co_player`, `co_tile`, `co_stamp`, `co_spike`, `co_heart`).
- No audio hooks yet; add SFX calls where `frame.flash`/`frame.shake` are called.
- Coin Collector hazard AI is simple (no pathing); the validator ignores mid-air ceilings.
- Cooking `stir` on desktop needs a mouse drag or holding RIGHT.

## Cooking reveal (round 8)

When the last step ends, the finished dish art grows to ~3.4x, centred, for 1.5 s (cap paused) with a treatment for the mean accuracy: 90-100 sparkle + warm glow (**CHEF'S KISS**), 50-90 plain with steam (**GOOD**), 30-50 tinted towards `earth1` with dark smoke (**A BIT BURNT**), 0-30 tinted towards `grass1` with a queasy wobble (**QUESTIONABLE**). Harness: `?cook=<accuracy>` forces every step's accuracy (`payload.debugAccuracy`).

## Workout: WarioWare-style micro-games; the hotel room and the hike chain three, everything else is one

`Workout` (payload `{ activity, city, day?, seed?, plan? }`). **Sessions (round 10):** `bands` (hotel room) plays a random combination of THREE of the eight hotel games and `hike` three from its pool (sprint, stretch, riverstones, pace, balance); every other activity is a single game. Every game runs once at normal speed (no x1.3 / x1.6 rounds any more). Each game opens with a READY card (its own name as the title, the command word, the instruction, the athlete demonstrating); between the games of a session the card is `frame.card()` (play clock paused, `active` false until READY, so the harness `ready()` hook works for every card). `plan: [ids]` forces the games for tests; the pick is seeded by `city|day`. **Titles:** the HUD title is the micro-game's own name from `META[id].name` (Push-ups, Plank, Jump Rope, City Run, Yoga…); the activity label ("Hotel room workout", "Trail run") is the small subtitle under the HUD with the city. Session score = mean of the games (unplayed games count 0 if it ends early); lives = 1 + `extraLives`, a game under 50% costs one, out of lives ends the session (score capped at 45). Cap 30 s for one game, 36 s for three.

## The handheld console (scene key `CarryOn`)

The Switch is now a proper handheld (the platformer cartridge is **Coin Collector**; its game id stays `carryon` and the scene key `CarryOn` so engine hooks and quick-play links keep working): a 23x20-tile screen (368x320) on top, a D-pad on the left, A/B on the right, START/SELECT pills. Every cartridge reads only the per-frame `Pad` snapshot from `console/input.ts` (`held`, `justPressed`, `justReleased`, `axisX/axisY`): multi-touch on the drawn zones (hold RIGHT and tap A with two fingers), keyboard (arrows, Z/SPACE = A, X = B, ENTER = START, SHIFT = SELECT) and a swipe-up over the screen = an A pulse. START pauses (the play cap pauses too).

Payload: `{ game?: 'carryon' | 'tetris' | 'heli', level?: ArcadeLevel, city, cityName?, hazard?, seed?, climate? }` (a raw `ArcadeLevel` still works; `{ level, city }` from the engine still works and boots Coin Collector). Boot: chime, then the cartridge's title card (name, city, 2 to 3 lines of instructions, the D-pad / A / B mapping for that game). It waits for START, A, or a tap on the screen; nothing auto-starts and the play cap is not running until then. The result card at the end holds the same way: score and verdict stay up until START, A, or the CONTINUE button, and `onDone` fires only then (the frame's result card; START / A on the pad trigger its CONTINUE). Same result card / `onDone` contract as every mini-game.

Cartridges live in `console/games/` behind `ConsoleGame` (`init(ctx, done)`, `update(dt, pad)`, `scoreNow()`, `destroy()`; `ctx` gives the screen rect, level, city, hazard, palette, seeded rng, difficulty, and `setHearts/setStatus/flash/shake/sfx`). `CONSOLE_GAMES` in `console/games/index.ts` is the slot.

**Coin Collector physics** (`carryonLevel.ts` `PHYS`): jump 330 / gravity 900 → apex 3.8 tiles (a 3-row rise clears with head room), flat air time × run 118 ≈ 5.4 tiles (a 4-tile gap is comfortable). Snow: jump 300 → 2-row steps. `validateLevel` mirrors these numbers.

**Courses** (`generateCourse(seed, { city, hazard, climate, screens? })`, round 7b): a flat intro with `S`, then segments of the five families 18 to 26 tiles wide stitched left to right in a seeded order (`staircase`, `towers`, `zigzag`, `islands` with `M` moving platforms over a wide pit, `bridges` with one-way / crumble spans over spikes), 2 to 3 tile pits carved in the ground between segments, a flat run-in and the flag `F` five tiles from the end. Coins are lines on platform tops, arcs over every pit, and two hidden clusters up high; after building, any coin the validator cannot reach is pruned (so courses are collectable by construction), and the flag must be reachable or the seed variant is retried. `validateLevel` now also checks the flag, treats `M` platforms as standable along their ±3-tile travel, and accepts a coin when a reachable standable cell is within 3 tiles sideways and one jump below (`coinReachable`). A hand-built level is used only if it contains a flag and validates; the engine's one-screen levels no longer act as templates. The screen has its own Phaser camera (viewport = screen, scroll starts at the screen origin) and the main camera ignores game objects, so scrolling never touches the bezel.

**One-screen levels** (`generateLevel(seed, { city, hazard, climate })`, kept for tests and non-course callers): a layout family per seed — `towers`, `staircase`, `zigzag`, `islands`, `bridges` (one-way `-` and crumble `C` spans over spike pits) — built with the physics' step height, then stamps are placed only on cells the validator's BFS can reach (farthest-point spread, at most one on the ground), hazard spawners `H` for fallers/flyers at the top or on ledges for walkers, a few spikes. Completable by construction; 20 attempts then the built-in level. The engine's `payload.seed` (or a hash of city|game) picks the level; hand-built levels are used as templates only when no seed is given and they still validate. Tiles: `#` solid, `.` empty, `S` start, `*` stamp, `H` spawner, `^` spikes, `-` one-way, `C` crumble (gives way 0.35 s after you stand on it).


## Work day puzzles (scene key `Work`, round 64)

Professor Layton style, themed to a software engineer at Uniswap. Payload `{ puzzle: Puzzle; pay: number; day: number }` (bank: `src/data/puzzles.json`; fallback samples in `work/sample.ts`). Page: illustrated frame (`work/puzzleArt.ts`, by `puzzle.art`: laptop default, pool, chart, code, chain, whiteboard), title, parchment prompt (drag to scroll when long), then four 50 px answer buttons (`kind: 'choice'`) or a keypad with display, DEL and ENTER (`kind: 'number'`, compared to 2 decimals). One HINT per puzzle (speech bubble). Wrong once: shake + "Not quite. One more try."; wrong twice: failed. The explain card (SOLVED / NOT TODAY, how it was solved, `explain`) holds until a tap, then the frame's result card holds until CONTINUE. No timer (`capSec` is effectively off). Score: 100 solved first try without hint (PERFECT), 60 solved with the hint or on the second try, 0 wrong twice (FAILED). Harness: `?work=solve|hint|fail[&puzzle=id][&stage=page|hint|explain]`; hooks `scene.answer(v)`, `scene.hint()`, `scene.tapExplain()`. Quick play: `?play=Work&puzzle=<id>` (needs `Work` registered in main.ts). Sheet: `e2e/layout/work.png`.
