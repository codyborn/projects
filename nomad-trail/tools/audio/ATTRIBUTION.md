## Sound effects

The 50 game effects are generated at runtime with [jsfxr](https://github.com/chr15m/jsfxr) (UNLICENSE, public domain); no files
are downloaded for them. The two exceptions are the recorded jet and wind clips listed above.

## Pack-Tris: why there is no famous falling-blocks theme

Cody suggested an archive.org upload of a "Tetris A Theme" 8-bit loop for Pack-Tris. That item carries no licence statement and
is a re-upload from a private Geometry Dash server, so its provenance cannot be established and it is not shipped. The melody
itself, **Korobeiniki**, is a 19th-century Russian folk tune in the public domain, so `tetris.tracker.korobeiniki` is our own
four-channel arrangement of it, written in `src/audio/tracker.ts`. That arrangement is the pick for the slot.

## Where the choices live

`src/audio/selection.json` maps every effect and music slot to a candidate id. The Sound Lab on the review hub
(`/trail/review/#sound`) auditions all candidates and exports that file's contents.
