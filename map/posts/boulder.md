I quietly exited my apartment at 4:00 am. My brother's car sat idling in the dark parking lot. There was a buzz from both the excitement of the hike to come and the lack of sleep. Although Bierstadt was only a day hike, Coloradans are fiends for their 14ers (mountains over 14k feet) and we had to get there early to beat the crowds. It was 4:30 am when we arrived at the trailhead parking lot and we were shocked to see the lot already full. We snagged the last spot as more cars piled into the queue behind us.

As we hiked in the darkness, we could see the sparkle of headlamps zig zagging up the silhouette of the mountain. I reeled as people already done with their hike passed us on their way down. We crossed marsh land and thick brush, eventually opening up to grassy hillsides. The alpenglow hinting of the upcoming sun colored the sky pink and blue. Pure white mountain goats, that resembled miniature abominable snowmen, covered the mountainside.

Once we got closer the summit, the terrain shifted and we scrambled over large boulders until we reached the top. The sun fully rose giving us a spectacular view of the valley behind us and surrounding mountain peaks. We ate a quick snack, snapped some shots of more goats and began our long trek back down.
